HOW IT IS COMPUTED
----------------------------------------
Source: per_page_results_{engine}.csv from nested kfold run
  (surrogate_multiengine_kfold_level1_fix_raw_g03757_nested_fixedtopk_perpage_v2)
  (surrogate_kfold_sroie_level1_fix_all616_g1_nested_fixedtopk_perpage_v2)

For each held-out page i (from the same outer fold):
  oracle_gap_i  = fixed_topk_k3_i - oracle_i
    = CER of fold-specific FixedTopK shortlist
      minus CER of best candidate among all 50
  surr_adv_i    = fixed_topk_k3_i - surr_k3_i
    = FixedTopK CER minus Surrogate K=3 CER
    (positive = surrogate beats FixedTopK for that page)

Key: ALL values (oracle, fixed_topk_k3, surr_k3) come from the
SAME nested kfold fold — no leakage, fully rigorous comparison.

Correlation: Spearman rank correlation between oracle_gap and surr_adv
across all held-out pages in the dataset.

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------
The per-image oracle gap significantly predicts surrogate advantage
on ALL 5 engine-dataset combinations (Spearman r=0.37-0.56, p<0.001).

The Vietnamese/SROIE difference is NOT a different mechanism — the
correlation structure is similar. The difference is in the DISTRIBUTION
of gap values:
  Vietnamese mean gap: 0.66-1.26pp → surrogate adv ≈ 0pp
  SROIE Tesseract mean gap: 4.07pp → surrogate adv +0.99pp

On Vietnamese Q4 pages (large gap, mean ~2.8pp),
surrogate DOES win: +0.52pp for Tesseract.
But Q4 is only 25% of pages; the majority have small gaps.
